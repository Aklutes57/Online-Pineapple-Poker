Questions

Is this real-money poker?

No. Everything here is play money. Chips have no value; the built-in ledger just helps you track who's up and who's down for bragging rights.

Do my friends need accounts?

No. They open your table link, type a nickname, and request a seat. As the host you approve each player and their buy-in. An account is optional — it just remembers your results, stats, friends list and table look between games.

What does signing up actually get me?

An all-time ledger of every session you've played, poker stats, a saved list of friends to auto-invite, a Discord webhook for your group, and a custom table look that follows you to every game you host.

What can the host control?

Blinds, action timer, pausing between hands, approving seats, topping up stacks, removing players, and closing the table.

What happens if I get disconnected?

Reopen the same link on the same device and you're back in your seat, cards intact. If it's your turn and the timer runs out, you auto-check or auto-fold and sit out until you're back.

How long does a table live?

A table stays open while people are playing and is cleaned up after an hour of inactivity. When the host closes it, the session ledger is the final word — and if you're signed in, that result is saved to your all-time ledger.
